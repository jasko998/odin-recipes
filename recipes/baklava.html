Baklava Recipe

Baklava is a rich, sweet dessert pastry made of layers of filo filled with chopped nuts and sweetened with syrup or honey.
It is a popular dessert in many Middle Eastern and Mediterranean cuisines.

Ingredients:

  1. 1 package of filo dough
  2. 2 cups of chopped nuts (walnuts, pistachios, or almonds)
  3. 1 cup of melted butter
  4. 1 cup of sugar
  5. 1 cup of water
  6. 1/2 cup of honey
  7. 1 teaspoon of lemon juice

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Roll out the filo dough and cut into strips.
  3. Layer the filo strips with the chopped nuts and butter.
  4. Bake for 30-35 minutes or until golden brown.
  5. Prepare the syrup by combining sugar, water, and honey in a pot.
  6. Bring the syrup to a boil and add the lemon juice.
  7. Once the baklava is done baking, pour the syrup over it.
  8. Let it cool before serving.

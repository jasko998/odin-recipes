Sheperd's Pie Recipe

Shepherd's Pie is a traditional British dish made with ground meat, vegetables, and topped with mashed potatoes.

Ingredients:

  1. 1 lb ground beef or lamb
  2. 1 onion, chopped
  3. 2 carrots, diced
  4. 1 cup frozen peas
  5. 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  6. 1 cup beef broth
  7. 4 cups mashed potatoes
  8. Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. In a large skillet, cook the ground meat until browned. Drain excess fat.
  3. Add the onion and carrots to the skillet and cook until softened.
  4. Stir in the tomato paste and beef broth. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Add the frozen peas and cook for another 5 minutes.
  6. Transfer the meat mixture to a baking dish and spread the mashed potatoes on top.
  7. Bake for 20-25 minutes or until the top is golden brown.

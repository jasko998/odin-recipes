Lasagna Recipe

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese.

Ingredients:

  1. 12 lasagna noodles
  2. 1 lb ground beef
  3. 2 cups marinara sauce
  4. 15 oz ricotta cheese
  5. 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  6. 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  7. 1 egg
  8. 2 cloves garlic, minced

Instructions:

  1. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package directions.
  2. In a large skillet, brown the ground beef and drain excess fat.
  3. Add the marinara sauce and simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. In a bowl, mix the ricotta cheese, egg, and garlic.
  5. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  6. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish.
  7. Place a layer of noodles on top of the sauce.
  8. Spoon some of the ricotta mixture over the noodles.
  9. Add a layer of meat sauce and shredded mozzarella cheese.
